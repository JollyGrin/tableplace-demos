Ardent Reapers → table.place

Paste a deck link (or bare deck id) from ardentreapers.com/decks for each seat. The demo fetches both decks from the game's own API, maps them to table.place packs in adapter.js, and seeds a two-seat duel lobby. View source — that's the tutorial.

Setup ritual (mulligan until two Creatures, opening discards, …) is played out on the table by the players, exactly as at a real one — see the README.
