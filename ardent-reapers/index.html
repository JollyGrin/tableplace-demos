Play Ardent Reapers online

Play the Ardent Reapers card game online — free, in your browser, no install, no account. Pick two decks from ardentreapers.com/decks, create a lobby, and you and your opponent share a two-player table on table.place, a digital tabletop: draw, flip, and move the real cards yourselves, exactly as you would across a real table.

Looking for an Ardent Reapers print and play? Skip the printer — the same decks you would sleeve at home are on the table in one click, with the game's own card scans.

Create a table

Paste a deck link (or bare deck id) from ardentreapers.com/decks for each seat, then send one seat link to your opponent.

No decks handy? Random decks drops in two community decks from ardentreapers.com so you can try a table right away.

Setup ritual (mulligan until two Creatures, opening discards, …) is played out on the table by the players, exactly as at a real one — see the README.

Ardent Reapers belongs to its creators — this is an unaffiliated community table, built on the deck data their site already serves. Find the game, its rules, and the deck builder at ardentreapers.com.
