Play Cataclysm Arcade online

Play the Cataclysm Arcade card game online — free, in your browser, no install, no account. Pick a booster pack or constructed deck from the-cade.com for each player, create a lobby, and you and your opponent share a two-player table on table.place, a digital tabletop: each Boss starts in play with its health counter, decks, discards, coin and level counters and token piles are dealt, and you play the real cards yourselves, exactly as you would across a real table.

Looking for a Cataclysm Arcade print and play? Skip the printer — the pack you would sleeve at home is on the table in one click, with the community's own card scans.

Create a table

Paste a pack or deck link (or a bare uuid) from the-cade.com for each seat — #/pack/… and #/deck/… links both work — then send one seat link to your opponent.

Draw your opening hand of 4 at the table; the fighter row is the felt in front of your Boss. Coins and Level start at 1, and the shared bags deal damage and +1 Attack counters — see the README for what the demo leaves to the players.

Cataclysm Arcade, its card data and art belong to Mothership Games — this is an unaffiliated community table built on the-cade.com, itself an unofficial fan tool. Find the game and how to play at cataclysmarcade.com.
